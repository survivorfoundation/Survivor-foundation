Who We Are

Survivor Charity Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to supporting vulnerable people and communities.

We believe every person deserves dignity, opportunity and hope.

Through education, healthcare, youth empowerment, community development and partnerships, we work to create sustainable positive change.

Survivor Charity Foundation was founded with a vision of creating hope and opportunities for vulnerable people.

The foundation is committed to serving communities through compassion, responsible leadership and sustainable initiatives.
