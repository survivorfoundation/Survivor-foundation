WHO WE ARE

Building stronger communities and brighter futures.

Survivor Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to supporting vulnerable people, empowering individuals, and strengthening communities through practical and sustainable initiatives.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, opportunity, hope, and the chance to contribute positively to their community.

Through collaboration with communities, volunteers, donors, institutions, and development partners, we work to transform challenges into opportunities for meaningful change.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable people and communities through access to support, opportunities, education, healthcare initiatives, partnerships, and sustainable community development.

Our Vision

A resilient and inclusive society where every person has the opportunity to live with dignity, hope, purpose, and equal opportunities for a better future.

Our Purpose

To turn compassion into action and create sustainable solutions that improve lives and strengthen communities.
