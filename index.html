About Survivor Foundation

Creating positive change through compassion and action.

Who We Are

Survivor Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to supporting vulnerable people and strengthening communities.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, opportunity, hope and the chance to build a better future.

Through education, healthcare initiatives, community support, youth empowerment and strategic partnerships, we work to create sustainable positive change.

Joshua John Baraba

Founder & CEO — Survivor Foundation

Joshua John Baraba is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Survivor Foundation, a community-focused organization dedicated to empowering vulnerable people and strengthening communities.

Through leadership, partnerships and community-focused initiatives, Survivor Foundation seeks to create hope, opportunities and sustainable positive change.

His leadership supports the foundation's commitment to education, healthcare initiatives, youth opportunities, community development and strategic partnerships.

🙏 PRAYERS

Prayers

Standing together in prayer for children in need, bringing hope, protection, strength and a brighter future.

🙏

For Orphans

Heavenly Father, we pray for children who have lost their parents and are growing up without the care and support of a family. Surround them with people who will love, protect, guide and encourage them. Give them hope for tomorrow and provide for their needs.

❤️

For Children with Disabilities

Lord, we pray for children living with disabilities. Give them strength, courage and opportunities to discover their abilities and reach their potential. Bless their families, caregivers, teachers and everyone who supports them.

🕊️

For Children in Need

God of hope, we pray for children facing poverty, hardship, neglect or difficult circumstances. Protect them from harm and open doors of opportunity. Provide food, education, healthcare, safety and people who will stand beside them.

🌍

For Their Future

Lord, we place the future of these children in Your hands. Give them wisdom, confidence, good health and opportunities to grow. Help us become a source of hope and positive change in their lives.

📖
“Let the little children come to me.”
— Matthew 19:14

Together in Prayer

May our prayers remind every child that they matter, their lives have value, and there is hope for a better tomorrow.
